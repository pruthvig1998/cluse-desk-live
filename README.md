## What you are looking at

A public, read-only snapshot of the **Cluse paper-trading bot**: the current book, the signals it is acting on, and what its agents are doing - refreshed automatically **every 5 minutes during the session**.

- **Live desk:** https://pruthvig1998.github.io/cluse-desk-live/
- **3D diagram of the system:** https://pruthvig1998.github.io/cluse-desk-live/3d/

The desk runs a Nasdaq-100 options research system on virtual money: technical setups, an intraday news radar, market-regime filters, and strict risk rules - conviction sizing, no chasing, take-profit and stop-loss exits, end-of-day flattening, and a drawdown kill-switch. Every rule is measured on paper first; the goal is a proven system before any real capital is ever risked.

## How this repo works

This repo holds only the published page and its data (the `data` branch). It is published automatically by the bot; nothing here is edited by hand. The core trading system itself is private - this is its public window.

## Disclaimer

Paper trading only - educational, not financial advice. No real orders, ever.
